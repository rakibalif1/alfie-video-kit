**ON SCREEN:** A CV document builds line by line; Chidi's old CV on the left with red crosses (photo, age 24, married, nationality, five pages); the new CV on the right with green ticks: "1 to 2 pages", "Name, phone, email, LinkedIn link", "No photo", "No date of birth, age, marital status or nationality", order cards "Profile, Experience, Education, Skills, newest first", an example achievement card "Served around 100 customers a day (only if true)", and a "TAILOR IT: use words from the job advert" magnet-highlight.
**NARRATION:**
N[calm]{0.3}: Now your CV. In the U.K., a CV is one or two pages. Not five.
C[worried]{0.2}: Mine has my photo, my age, and five pages.
K[calm]{0.2}: We will fix that.
N[calm]{0.3}: At the top, put your name, phone number, email, and your LinkedIn link. Do not put a photo. Do not put your date of birth, your age, your marital status, or your nationality.
N[calm]{0.3}: Then, in this order: a short profile, your work experience, your education, and your skills. Newest first.
N[excited]{0.3}: Under each job, show what you achieved, with numbers. Not 'I worked in a shop'. Say 'I served around one hundred customers a day', if that is true.
N[worried]{0.4}: And most important: change your CV for every job. Use the words from the job advert.
