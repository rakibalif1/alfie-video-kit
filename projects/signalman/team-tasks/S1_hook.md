SCENE 'hook' (~65 s). The end told first. (0-20 s) twilight over the cutting after the accident: blue-grey, still; thin steam drifts out of the tunnel mouth; the red lamp pulses; far off a stopped locomotive silhouette with 4 to 5 tiny moving lantern dots near the tunnel (the consequences, implied only). (20-40 s) slow push toward the narrator, a coated silhouette with hat standing at the cutting edge, head bowed, breath fog; the red lamp below him. (40-65 s, 'let me take you back') the light slowly rewinds: lanterns dim and vanish, steam drifts back INTO the tunnel, sky brightens to pale afternoon grey, ending on the empty edge of the cutting and the calm red lamp far below.
