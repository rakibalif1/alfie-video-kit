SCENE 'story' (~190 s). The night story at the fire, with three faint memory visions. Base: the box interior at night (two silhouettes by the stove, wind howling in the tunnel: mist drifting). (0-30 s) interlude as in S3 but darker. (30-75 s) VISION 1, a year ago: the tunnel mouth at night with the red lamp; the GHOST appears at the lamp (pale translucent figure, left arm across face, right arm waving frantically, cold white rim glow), the picture shakes lightly; then an orange glow blooms inside the tunnel with a silent expanding shock ring and tiny lamp dots rushing: the crash implied. (75-110 s) interlude: the narrator's calm reasoning, firelight, the signalman shaking his head. (110-150 s) VISION 2: dusk, the ghost at the lamp again; then a train of lit carriage windows slides slowly past, one window with a motionless seated woman; stillness. (150-190 s) VISION 3 (now): the ghost at the lamp, slowly waving; on the box wall the bell hangs; the picture ends on the bell beginning to sway gently.
