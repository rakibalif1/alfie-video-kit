SCENE 'box' (~120 s). The signal box. Interior cutaway of a narrow wooden hut: stove fire, desk with ledger, brass telegraph instrument (needle ticks side to side), a small bell on the wall (silent), a row of lever handles (red and blue tops), oil lamp, window showing rails and the red lamp, rain-darkened planks. Two silhouettes: the signalman seated at his desk (beard, heavy brows) and the narrator standing then seated. (0-35 s) arrival, handshake refused: the signalman stands without moving; stove flicker; (35-85 s) the talk: slow push-in on the signalman's face lit amber from the side, hands folded; the telegraph needle ticks; (85-120 s) the narrator rises to leave at the door, dusk in the doorway; the signalman raises a hand-lamp as a signal; window shows the red lamp pulsing. Smoke from the stove pipe, dust motes.
