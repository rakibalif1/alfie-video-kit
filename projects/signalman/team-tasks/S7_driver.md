SCENE 'driver' (~100 s). What the driver said. (0-30 s) the lamp-lit group in the cutting at night; a man in a blue coat (silhouette) takes the narrator by the arm; then the signal box interior: a cloth-covered shape on the floor with boots visible, the lamp, the stove dead, quiet. (30-70 s) the driver seated on the bank with head in hands, lamp beside him, steam drifting; as he speaks, an amber FLASHBACK glows in the air above him: morning, the tunnel mouth at dawn, the signalman small with his back to the tunnel looking at his red lamp; a locomotive emerges from the tunnel (trainHeadOn, growing, lamps flaring); in its cab the driver's silhouette throws one arm across his eyes and waves the other: EXACTLY the ghost's pose; the signalman turns too late; a soft white flash, then silence. (70-100 s) back to the night: the narrator's silhouette, motionless; very faintly the ghost pose is superimposed on the driver's pose (translucent), then fades.
