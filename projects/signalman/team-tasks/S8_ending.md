SCENE 'ending' (~95 s). The last turn. (0-35 s) replay the very first image: top of the cutting, the narrator silhouette calling down; the signalman far below looks up with dread; now a faint cold rim light outlines the narrator so HE resembles the ghost, and a translucent copy of the ghost pose briefly overlaps him. (35-70 s) years later: the narrator alone on a hill at dusk above a long straight railway line far away across dark fields, mist in the valley, one distant red lamp point, wind in grass, a very faint steam plume of a far train; he stands with his back half turned. (70-95 s) he walks away, never looking round; the camera lingers on the line; the red lamp pulses once, twice, and fades out last; hold on black for a moment.
