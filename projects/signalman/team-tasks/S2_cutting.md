SCENE 'cutting' (~100 s). Looking down into the deep cutting from the top. (0-30 s) tall wet clay walls (cuttingWalls) converging, a zigzag path drawn down the left wall, rails far below converging to the tunnel portal, a shaft of weak sun on one wall, drifting fog, dripping water glints; narrator silhouette at the top edge calling down; far below the signalman (small, back to us, beside the red lamp) looking at the tunnel. (30-55 s, 'He turned. But he did not look up') he turns toward the tunnel mouth, holds, then slowly lifts his head up the cutting. (55-100 s, the descent) the camera/layers move DOWN the zigzag: walls rise around, light thins, fog thickens, rails grow larger; end near the bottom: the signalman as a bearded silhouette facing us with a dread-filled stillness (a cold rim light on his face, red glow on the ground).
